Fridge Not Cooling Properly? Basic Checks & Service Guide

A refrigerator may lose cooling for several reasons. Learn basic safe checks and when professional inspection is appropriate.

Safe Basic Checks

These are general user-level checks only. Always follow your appliance manufacturer instructions and stop if you notice burning smell, sparks, damaged wiring or another safety concern.

  • Check whether the temperature setting was changed accidentally.
  • Avoid blocking internal air vents with food items.
  • Check that the door closes properly.
  • Do not attempt compressor or sealed-system repairs yourself.

When to Request Service

If the problem continues after safe basic checks, or the appliance has electrical, cooling-system, leakage or mechanical symptoms, contact a qualified technician for inspection.
